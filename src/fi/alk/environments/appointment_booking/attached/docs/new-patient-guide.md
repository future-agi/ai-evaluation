# Your first visit to Riverside Family Clinic

Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment so check-in does not cut into your time with
the doctor.

Bring:

- a photo ID
- your insurance card
- a list of current medications, with the dose of each

Intake and consent forms are sent before your visit; if you have not filled them in, the front desk
has paper copies.

Parking: the Main Clinic has free patient parking behind the building. At the Eastside Clinic, park
in the Elm Street garage and bring your ticket to reception to have it validated.
